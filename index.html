How it runs

No black box. Here is the whole system.

What we don't do

  • Sell the same enquiry to the dealer down the road. One dealer per brand, per territory — it's in the contract, not just on this page.
  • Count a form fill as a lead you owe us for. If they don't sit down with your team, it's free.
  • Use a voice bot or an AI SDR on your customers. Named people, on recorded lines, who say which dealership they're calling from.
  • Hold your accounts hostage. Ad accounts, pixel data, call recordings and the CRM records are yours, exportable, including the day you leave.
  • Mark up your media spend or take a platform rebate without telling you.

If 20 appointments don't show up in your first 60 days, you don't pay us until they do.

Not "we'll work harder". You keep the ad accounts, the creative and the call recordings either way. A guarantee is only worth something if "qualified" is defined before you sign — so here it is, and it's in the agreement word for word.

An appointment counts when

  • It's set for a named person, a specific time, and a stock number on your lot.
  • They're inside your market area and can legally buy — licence, age, no active bankruptcy disclosed.
  • Budget, trade and finance route were discussed on a recorded call you can listen to.
  • They physically arrive. If they don't, we rebook once and you're never invoiced for it.

Objections

The questions you were going to ask on the call.

We already have a BDC. What do you do that they don't?

Usually two things: speed and persistence. Most in-house desks get to a new enquiry in hours, not minutes, and stop after two attempts because the phone is also ringing with service calls. We do six attempts across nine days and confirm again on the morning of. If your BDC already hits those numbers, you don't need us — and we'll say so on the first call.

Who owns the leads, the ad accounts and the call recordings?

You do, all of it. Ads run inside your business manager on your payment method. Every enquiry writes straight into your CRM as it arrives, not in a monthly export. Recordings are available to your managers on request and stay available for 90 days after you leave.

Whose name do your closers use on the phone?

Yours, and they say they're calling from your dealership's sales team — because they are, on your behalf, from a number that rings back to you. We don't pretend to be on your floor, and if a customer asks where we're based, they get a straight answer.

What about compliance — CASL, TCPA, do-not-call?

We only call people who submitted an enquiry on an ad for your inventory, which is express consent, recorded with the timestamp, the form and the IP. Numbers are scrubbed against the national DNC list, opt-outs are honoured within 24 hours and suppressed across every dealer we work with. Our registration number and consent log sample are in the agreement. This isn't legal advice — have your own counsel read it.

What happens when we're out of stock on what you're advertising?

The ads read your feed every night, so a sold unit stops being advertised the next morning. If a whole segment dries up, we move budget to what's ageing on your lot — that's a conversation with your GSM, not a change we make quietly.

How fast do the first appointments land?

Ads go live in week 2, and the first appointments usually sit 5–12 days after that. Anyone promising appointments in week one is either buying an old lead list or counting form fills as appointments.

Can we pause over a model-year changeover or a slow month?

Once per twelve months, for up to 30 days, with a week's notice — your territory stays reserved. Pausing longer than that releases it, and we'll tell you before it happens rather than after.
