How it runs

No black box. Here is the whole system.

If an agency won't show you the mechanism, they're selling you hope. This is every moving part, in order, with the week it happens.

Day 0

Fit call, 20 minutes

We check three things: deal size above £4k, capacity to take 8+ calls a month, and an offer that survives a cold inbox. Roughly half the companies we speak to get a no — usually because the offer needs work first, and we'll tell you what.

Week 1

Build, before a single send

We interview your closer, read your last 20 won and lost deals, and build the list from that — not a bought database. Four sending domains bought and warmed for 14 days. You approve every sequence in writing.

Week 3+

Send, then actually reply

Sequences go out in small daily batches. Every reply is answered by a named person on our team inside 2 working hours — no autoresponder, no bot. Objections get logged so month two's copy is written against real pushback, not guesses.

Ongoing

Handover you can walk into

A booked call arrives in your calendar with the full email thread, the company brief, who else sits on the decision, and the line that got the reply. Everything syncs to your CRM. The data is yours, exportable, on day one or day four hundred.

What we don't do

  • Resell you a list we sold to three of your competitors. One client per sector, per city.
  • Send AI-written emails unread. Drafting is assisted; a named person edits and signs off every sequence before it sends.
  • Scrape personal inboxes, or email anyone outside a legitimate business-interest basis.
  • Hold your data hostage. Lists, sequences and replies export on request, including the day you leave.
  • Bill for "strategy", "brand awareness" or hours. You pay for meetings.

If we don't book 10 qualified calls in 60 days, month three is free.

Not "we'll work harder". You keep the domains, the lists and the sequences either way. The guarantee is only worth something if "qualified" is defined before you sign — so here it is, and it's in the contract too.

A call counts as qualified when

  • The attendee can sign, or sits in the room with whoever signs.
  • The company matches the profile you signed off — size, sector, geography.
  • They know what you do and roughly what it costs before the call starts.
  • They attend. If they don't, we rebook once and you're not charged.

Objections

The questions you were going to ask on the call.

Isn't cold email dead?

Untargeted volume is dead — our reply rate on bought lists was 0.4%, which is why we stopped using them. Researched lists with a specific, checkable reason for the email still work; that's the entire job, and it's why we cap how many clients we take.

Do you use AI to write the emails?

For first drafts and research, yes. Nothing sends without a named person rewriting it — you'll see who, and you approve every sequence in writing before it goes out. If an email reads like it was generated, it doesn't ship; that's the fastest way to get a domain burned and a buyer annoyed.

What happens to our domain reputation?

We never send from your primary domain. We buy and warm lookalike domains (yourbrand-hq.com), configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and keep each inbox under 30 sends a day. If a domain's deliverability drops, it's retired and replaced at our cost.

Is this GDPR compliant?

We email named business roles at companies on a legitimate-interest basis, with the identity of the sender, the source of the data and a working opt-out in every message. Opt-outs are suppressed permanently across every client. We're registered with the ICO, number ZA123456, and our data processing agreement is signed before any of your data moves. Not legal advice — have your own counsel review it.

When do the first calls land?

First sends go out in week 3 after domain warm-up. Typically the first booked call lands 10–20 days after that. Anyone promising meetings in week one is either sending from your main domain or working a list they've already burned.

Who actually works on our account?

Two named people — the strategist who writes your copy and the person who answers replies. You get both on a shared Slack channel. No account manager relaying messages, and no offshore team you weren't told about.

What if it doesn't work?

The guarantee above covers the commercial side. Beyond that: after 90 days you can leave with 30 days' notice, keep every asset, and we'll write you an honest handover on what worked and what didn't. We'd rather that than a client who stays and resents it.
