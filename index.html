What we don't do

  • Sell the same buyer to the dealer down the road. One dealer per brand, per territory.
  • Put a voice bot on your customers. Named people, recorded lines.
  • Claim a deal your own floor traffic brought in. See the definition below.
  • Keep your accounts. Ads, pixels, recordings and CRM records are yours, including the day you leave.
  • Mark up media or pocket a platform rebate.

No sale, no invoice. Here's what counts as a sale.

"Pay per deal" is only worth something if the deal is defined before you sign. This wording is in the agreement.

We invoice when all four are true

  • The buyer came from an appointment we booked, logged in your CRM with the source.
  • A unit was delivered and the contract funded — not signed, funded.
  • They weren't already in your CRM in the 90 days before we called.
  • Unwound, cancelled or returned deals are credited back in full.

Objections

Questions you'd ask on the call.

How do you make money if nobody buys?

We don't — which is why we turn down dealers whose gross or floor capacity won't carry it. We're funding the ads and the phone work up front, so we only take rooftops we expect to sell for.

We already have a BDC. What's different?

Speed and persistence: first dial in minutes, six attempts across nine days, and a confirmation call the morning of. If your desk already does that, you don't need us.

Who owns the leads and the ad accounts?

You do. Ads run in your business manager on your card. Enquiries write into your CRM as they arrive. Recordings stay available for 90 days after you leave.

How do we agree which deals you get paid for?

Your CRM decides, not us. Every appointment is logged with its source before the visit, and we invoice from your own delivered-and-funded report. Disputed? It comes off the invoice.

What about CASL, TCPA and do-not-call?

We only call people who submitted an enquiry on an ad for your stock — express consent, logged with timestamp, form and IP. Numbers scrubbed against the national DNC list; opt-outs honoured within 24 hours. Not legal advice — have your counsel read the agreement.

How fast do the first cars move?

Ads live in week 2, first appointments 5–12 days later, first deliveries usually inside 30 days. Anyone promising sales in week one is counting form fills.
