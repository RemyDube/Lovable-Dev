The job

Four things, all day.

1

2

A good listing gets messages within minutes. You reply from your business number and the CRM — whoever answers first usually gets the buyer.

Book the appointment

4

Get paid

Straight talk

It suits some people and not others.

Apply if

  • You'd rather message and call people all day than stand on a showroom floor.
  • You answer fast, and you don't mind that buyers message in the evening.
  • You want what you earn tied to what you do, not to hours logged.
  • You can keep yourself going without someone standing over you.

Don't apply if

  • You need a guaranteed paycheque this month. There's no hourly here.
  • You want to be offline at 5pm. That's when most buyers start messaging.
  • You expect leads handed to you. You post the cars; the messages come from your listings.
  • Being self-employed and setting aside your own tax doesn't suit you.

Questions

Before you apply.

Do I have to close the sale?

No. Your job finishes at a booked appointment with the details in the CRM. The dealership's floor team handles the test drive, the numbers and the paperwork.

Do I need car sales experience?

It helps, but you're not the one closing. What matters is answering quickly, asking the right questions and keeping your follow-ups tidy. If you've sold anything over a phone or a keyboard before, you'll recognise the work.

Do I pay for ads or anything else?

No. Marketplace listings are free to post, and you never pay us for anything — no licence fee, no CRM fee, no training package.

When and how do I get paid?

Once the vehicle is sold and confirmed, you invoice for it and we pay out twice a month. You're self-employed, so nothing is deducted at source — put your own tax aside as you go.

What hours am I expected to work?

Your call, with one condition: you have to overlap with when people are actually shopping for a car. In practice that's evenings and weekends more than nine-to-five. Nobody sets your shifts.

What do I need to have?

A phone, a computer and reliable internet. We supply the number, the CRM, the photos and the accounts. You need to be in Canada and able to invoice as a self-employed contractor.

Is there a limit on how many I can do?

No cap. The limit is how many cars you can get listed and how quickly you get back to people. Post more, answer faster, book more.
